Formatting rules and in-game test
  • 130-character budget includes all colour codes. Count uses JavaScript UTF-16 length; non-ASCII behaviour needs in-game verification.
  • Green openings and levels; yellow events and punchlines; white locations and calls to join. No red, no blue and no custom hexadecimal colours.
  • Area names and closing calls to action are omitted. Coordinates retain their original digits and decimal precision.
  • Location uses [World N(x,y)] without colour codes inside. Automatic area insertion and clickability must be tested in game.
  • Missing world/coordinates or oversized output disables Copy. No essential details are silently truncated.
Based on Janne's MIT-licensed Once Human Chat Colorizer. See original project. Invite formatter is an experimental addition.
